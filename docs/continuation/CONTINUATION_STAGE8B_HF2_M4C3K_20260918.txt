WHAT THE NINE M4C3K TESTS PROVE

1. Crash after durable ResolutionExecutionReceipt, before presentation attempt
A real child process creates the native-selected resolution and lets the trusted text response completion create the durable ResolutionExecutionReceipt.
The child exits before present_native_utterance.
A new process on the same isolated SQLite database recovers the pending executed speech act.
No NativePresentationRecord, Room audio artifact or NativeAudioTransportReceipt appears automatically.
Repeated restart still does not replay presentation.

2. Crash after native voice choice, before artifact publication
The native voice selector returns a genuine test-fixture voice choice, then the process hard-exits before _publish_bytes.
After restart:
- the text execution receipt remains;
- no audio artifact exists;
- no NativePresentationRecord exists;
- no audio transport receipt exists.
The in-memory voice decision is not promoted into durable execution/presentation evidence.

3. Crash after audio artifact publication, before NativePresentationRecord durability
The process publishes the audio artifact and hard-exits before append_native_presentation.
After restart:
- exactly one Room audio artifact may survive;
- the artifact remains cognitive_authority=false;
- no NativePresentationRecord is fabricated from the artifact;
- no NativeAudioTransportReceipt is fabricated;
- repeated restart does not promote the orphan artifact.

This verifies the awkward partial state explicitly rather than hiding it.

4. Crash after NativePresentationRecord durability, before any audio fetch
The process persists the exact linked NativePresentationRecord and hard-exits.
After restart:
- exactly one linked presentation remains;
- it still points to the same ResolutionExecutionReceipt;
- source_resolution_execution_confirmed=true;
- no audio transport receipt exists until a later actual fetch;
- repeated restart does not duplicate the presentation.

5. At-most-once speech-act consequence learning after every crash boundary
For each of the four crash phases:
- a later admissible conversational USER_MESSAGE may consume the persisted resolution outcome;
- clarify consequence observations increment exactly once;
- transition observations increment exactly once;
- pending resolution state clears after consumption;
- a second later message does not learn the same executed outcome again.

Presentation/artifact crash state therefore does not create duplicate speech-act execution or duplicate consequence learning.

6. Audio fetch after restart remains transport-only
After recovering an already-durable NativePresentationRecord:
- a real M4C3I _NativeAudioRoomResponse send may create exactly one NativeAudioTransportReceipt;
- playback_confirmed remains false;
- speech-act action observations do not change;
- speech-act transition observations do not change;
- the still-pending text execution remains the text execution provenance until a later conversational outcome consumes it.
