WHY M4C3H WAS NEEDED
M4C3G proved clean true-process restart from durable SQLite state, including replayed already-consumed receipt recovery.
It did not prove behavior when the process terminates abruptly around execution/receipt persistence.

M4C3H therefore tests hard-exit windows with os._exit, isolated SQLite databases and brand-new subprocess/runtime instances.
The central distinction is deliberate:
- a missing durable receipt is not permission to learn;
- after a content send window, a missing durable receipt is also NOT evidence that no bytes were sent;
- process-local dispatch capabilities do not survive restart;
- restart must not silently replay old execution;
- a durably persisted receipt may be restored and consumed later at most once.

VERIFIED SEMANTICS
- Crash before trusted content send completion creates no durable execution receipt.
- Crash after content send completion but before receipt durability creates no learning authority.
- Absence of a durable receipt in that content window is not a no-send assertion.
- A new runtime does not possess the previous process-local dispatch capability.
- The runtime does not automatically resend pre-crash resolution content on restart.
- A durable content receipt restores its linked pending expression/speech-act provenance and later outcome consumption remains at most once.
- A pre-durability non-content crash leaves no durable non-content receipt.
- A durable defer/remain-silent receipt restores the no-content pending state, blocks duplicate local execution, and later consequence consumption remains at most once.
