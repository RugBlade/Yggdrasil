DISPOSABLE DESIGN CONTRACT v1
A. Unique proposition ledger
Identity is the complete six-field proposition_key: subject, relation, object, polarity, universal, modality. Confidence and surface serialization are not proposition identity.
Each admitted input observation retains its available source fields: source label, source_record_id, source_memory_id(s), modifiers and input-derived flag. Source IDs are SHA-256 of canonical structural JSON. Numeric confidence readings are retained separately; exact duplicate observations collapse, differing readings or distinct source IDs remain visible.
Missing occurrence/source identity is not invented from list order. The prototype preserves available LogicalProposition provenance; integrating the richer current/bounded/persistent envelopes before reasoning.py's premise_map is an explicit remaining compatibility task.
Unique proposition identity carries no aggregate confidence. NodeView.confidence is None, explicitly absent; it is not zero, a selected route value, or native abstention.

B. Direct proof ledger
Each record contains formal rule, role-ordered premise keys, conclusion key, explicit rule-enabling evidence keys where relevant, and all available premise/evidence source IDs.
Fingerprint: SHA-256 over canonical JSON of version=1, rule, premise_keys, conclusion_key, rule_evidence_keys, premise_source_ids and rule_evidence_source_ids.
Ordered premise slots preserve rule roles. Source lists are set-canonicalized. Confidence readings and recursive producer IDs do not enter the fingerprint. Identical routes cannot multiply through replay or cycles. Distinct rules/premise identities/conclusions or provenance bundles remain distinguishable.
Parallel observations supporting an identical premise are retained within that premise's support inventory; they do not fabricate additional formal rule applications or path products.
The five existing formal rule families retain their guards. Explicit non-built-in transitivity declarations are retained as rule-enabling provenance; their absence cannot silently pass the proof audit.
No arbitrary proof paths are enumerated. A direct record points to proposition identities; producer references are attached after the finite direct-record set is formed. Cycles therefore form finite references rather than recursive fingerprint expansion.

FINITE BUDGET / COMPLETENESS CONTRACT
Separate nonnegative integer parameters: max_conclusion_steps (default 128 new unique identities) and max_proof_records (default 1024 direct records). These are prototype resource defaults, not semantic thresholds or a frozen runtime configuration.
Closure uses synchronous whole frontiers. If the next frontier exceeds remaining conclusion capacity, no preferred subset is admitted; the complete frontier is recorded as blocked and closure_complete=false. This makes bounded closure independent of premise order without lexical or route preference. Exact equality with the budget is allowed; a final no-new-frontier check distinguishes complete from truncated.
Proof enumeration follows closure and cannot consume conclusion capacity. If at most max_proof_records distinct direct records exist, they are retained. On the first cap+1 distinct record, proof enumeration stops; no preferred prefix is exposed, records is empty, proof_record_lower_bound=cap+1, and proof_enumeration_complete_for_known=false. The lower bound is not claimed to be the final total.
proof_ledger_complete = closure_complete AND proof_enumeration_complete_for_known. This status is global and conservative; the prototype makes no finer per-target completeness claim.
An incomplete ledger supplies no proof-eligible query content through the prototype adapter. It does not claim complete evidence and does not select any speech act. This is a provenance eligibility boundary, not a programmed remain-silent choice.
max_conclusion_steps=1 can retain one conclusion plus both independent proofs with proof capacity 2. Reducing only proof capacity to 1 leaves proposition closure unchanged and marks proofs incomplete.
Finite means finite structural enumeration over admitted inputs and bounded new identities; it is not a claim of constant-memory or adversarial-performance certification. The prototype has no legacy max_steps alias; exact runtime call compatibility remains to be designed.
