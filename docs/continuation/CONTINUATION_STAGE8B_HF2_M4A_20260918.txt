M4A mechanism:
1. Programmed resolution affordances are bounded to clarify / ask / defer / remain-silent. They are actuator possibilities only, never preferences.
2. Ambiguity does NOT directly choose clarify. Clarify is only eligible when unresolved ambiguity exists AND native question content already exists.
3. remain-silent is an affordance, not a default fallback.
4. A single eligible affordance cannot be reported as Yggdrasil's choice, because infrastructure would have forced it.
5. Missing learned consequence geometry for any eligible act leaves selection unresolved.
6. Exact native-geometry ties remain unresolved.
7. A native-choice claim is allowed only when at least two eligible acts have observed consequence geometry and one predicted post-state has a unique minimum under the injected native geometry.
8. The selector generates no utterance text and has no semantic-truth, answer, content-generation, cognitive-memory, relationship, source-write or deployment authority.
9. Runtime integration must inject the already-computed post-closure native state from Environment -> IRG -> DKT -> regional EGR -> RL/Frenet -> DKT and the actual DKT transport/distance/tie machinery. The carrier does not imitate those mechanisms.
