M4C3H QUESTION
M4C3G proves clean true-process restart after durable state.
M4C3H asks what happens if the process terminates abruptly at specific execution-persistence boundaries.

For content clarify/ask:
- ASGI terminal response send can return before complete_resolution_dispatch persists a ResolutionExecutionReceipt.
- A crash after send completion but before durable receipt append must not be relabeled as no-send.
- On restart, absence of a durable receipt must grant no learning authority and must not auto-resend an old process-local dispatch.
- If the receipt is durable before the crash, restart must restore it and later consume it at most once.

For non-content defer/remain-silent:
- there is no outward transport side effect;
- a crash before receipt append must leave no durable execution receipt;
- a crash after receipt append must restore the receipt, block duplicate local execution, and allow at-most-once later consequence consumption.
