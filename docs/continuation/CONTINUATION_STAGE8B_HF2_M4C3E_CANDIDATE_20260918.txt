M4C3E BOUNDED DESIGN
Problem exposed after M4C3D:
- QUD/proposition/proof alternatives are typed and preserved but remain inventory-only.
- Existing clarify eligibility only counts reference/event-role content.
- Multiple proof-supported answers are emitted through the ordinary response path, so proposition alternatives cannot participate in a native resolution act.
- Simply mapping parser/reasoner multiplicity to "clarify" would violate the native-choice boundary.

Candidate solution:
1. Keep reference/event-role units act-specific where they already have explicit validated resolution semantics.
2. Make QUD/proposition/proof typed alternative units ACT-NEUTRAL carriers:
   compatible_actions = ("clarify", "ask")
   their own act field is empty before selection.
3. Act-neutral unresolved content may make BOTH question-like actuators feasible together.
   It never selects between ask and clarify.
4. resolution_affordances gains an act_neutral_alternative_count substrate input.
   A nonzero count can add BOTH ask and clarify to the eligible set.
   Programmed eligibility remains zero speech-act-choice authority.
5. choose_resolution_act remains unchanged:
   a native choice still requires at least two independently eligible affordances,
   observed consequence geometry for every eligible act,
   equal-affordance native reference coverage,
   finite native DKT distances,
   and one unique native H^s minimum.
   Missing observations or exact ties remain unresolved.
6. Only AFTER a genuine native ask/clarify selection may compatible QUD/proposition/proof units be copied into NativeResolutionContent with the selected act.
7. Language realization for act-neutral alternative sets uses only already-admitted option strings plus punctuation.
   No "Do you mean", "Which one", or other canned clarification text is added.
   Ask versus clarify changes provenance/frame only, not authored fallback wording.
8. Multiple proof-supported answers remain ordinary output unless learned native consequence geometry actually selects a resolution act.
   Multiplicity alone cannot suppress the ordinary response.
9. Once a genuine native resolution act is selected, ordinary multi-answer transport is not used as a fallback.
   If independent expression selection is private/unresolved, or no supported adapter executes, no ordinary-answer fallback is substituted.
10. Existing authenticated-owner Room HTTP remains the only content-send execution-receipt adapter.
    ASGI send completion remains nonsemantic evidence only.
11. Receipt alone still does not train.
    Later consequence learning still requires a distinct later conversational USER_MESSAGE plus existing native admissibility/security/nonworsening gates.
