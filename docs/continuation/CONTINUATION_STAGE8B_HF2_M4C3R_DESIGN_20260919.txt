- derivative content unit:

STRUCTURAL CAUSE
conversation_resolution.native_current_turn_alternative_sources() first emits direct unresolved-source units such as:
- reference-alternatives;
- event-role-alternatives.
It then emits qud-alternatives for question-scoped unresolved sources.
The QUD unit records structural provenance through source_units including:
unresolved-source:<source-id>.

NativeLanguageFaculty realization currently renders every eligible resolution-content unit independently.
Therefore a primary unresolved source and its derivative QUD wrapper may become two surface clauses even when:
- they refer to the same unresolved source;
- they preserve the same alternative set;
- the wrapper contributes no independent ambiguity.

PROVISIONAL TRANSPARENT SURFACE PRINCIPLE

1. unit.kind == qud-alternatives;
